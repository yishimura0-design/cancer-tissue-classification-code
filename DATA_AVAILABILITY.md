# Data availability and access

The underlying microscopy datasets are **not included** in this repository. They are not owned by the code publisher, and no right to redistribute them is asserted.

An authorized data custodian/corresponding author must determine whether, to whom, and under what permissions the original data may be shared. Reproducing the publication's numerical results requires lawful access to the **same original images, labeling/QC decisions, biological grouping identifiers and frozen fold assignments**. A new dataset may be used to evaluate the software but will not reproduce the original published statistics.

The repository may supply **synthetic example data** solely for code smoke tests. Such files must not be confused with the private research images or clinical/biological ground truth.

Do not commit source TIFFs, paired BF/ED images, image caches, sample-level manifests, paths tied to specific users, original predictions, or model checkpoints.
